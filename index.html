Questions, answered

Common things people ask before starting a project with us.

How do I book a site visit?

Message us on WhatsApp or call the numbers below with your area and rough requirement — we'll fix a time to come measure your space, free of cost.

How long does a modular kitchen or wardrobe take to complete?

A single kitchen or wardrobe usually takes 2–4 weeks from final measurement to fitting, depending on size and material availability. Full home interiors take longer and we'll give you a clear timeline in the quote.

Do you provide after-sale service?

Yes — hinges, drawer channels and shutters are covered for a period after installation, and we come back to fix issues that arise from normal use.

Can I choose my own materials and finishes?

Yes. We'll show you material and finish options during the design stage and build to whatever you approve — you're not limited to a fixed catalogue.

Do you only work in Assandh, or nearby areas too?

We're based in Assandh and take on projects across Karnal and nearby towns. Message us your location and we'll confirm.
